WinkTPG is an execution framework for Multi-Agent Path Finding (MAPF) that refines discrete MAPF plans into kinodynamically feasible speed profiles while reasoning about temporal constraints and execution uncertainty.

## Highlights

* Published in **IEEE Transactions on Automation Science and Engineering (T-ASE)**, 23, 9162-9175, 2026
* Will present at **IROS 2026**
* Generates speed profiles for up to 1,000 agents within 1 second
* Improves solution quality by up to 51.7% over existing MAPF execution methods
* Validated in high-fidelity physics-based simulation and on real-world robots
